Program Objective

Praxis is driven by the purpose of creating resources that will lead India’s transformation in the digital world.

These leaders will be value-driven, embrace diversity, be tech and data-savvy, communicate effectively, and have the agility to respond skilfully to the rapidly changing global environment.

The Praxis PGDM empowers students with the knowledge, skills, analytical ability and management perspectives needed to provide leadership in an increasingly digital and competitive world.

Praxis provides an environment that is conducive to learning and growing as individuals. We give you the freedom to think, question, discuss, debate, absorb, experiment – learn by doing and learn without fear.

The PGDM program is designed to achieve the following specific objectives:

PEO1: Apply multidisciplinary quantitative and qualitative techniques, and knowledge of digital technologies, to analyze business scenarios

PEO2: Work effectively in teams, demonstrate leadership and data-driven decision-making skills

PEO3: Integrate theory and practice to find sustainable solutions to strategic business problems

PEO4: Demonstrate effective written and oral communication skills

PEO5: Develop cultural sensitivity and exhibit a high degree of integrity and ethics in behaviour
